# Albanian voice assistant

*Raised by Eri, 2026-09-11. Status: idea, for later. Part of Phase 4 in `docs/CONTEXT.md`.*

## The idea

A small assistant beside each role's screen, on phone and web, that **speaks Albanian** and
that you can **speak to in Albanian**: it turns Albanian audio into text, gives you a recap, or a
briefing. Something to reach slowly, not now.

## Notes from the discussion

**Why it fits:** on site, typing on a phone (dirty hands, in a hurry) is the real obstacle to
logging costs. Voice removes it.

**The simplest valuable first step:** the technician records a voice note in Albanian ("sot
përdorëm 20 thasë çimento dhe 3 punëtorë") → the app turns it into text and fills in the day's
log → they check and confirm. Directly saves time on the core problem.

**Then, step by step:**

- A daily **recap** for the engineer ("sot në Astir: …").
- A **briefing** for the owner across projects.
- The assistant **speaking back** in Albanian, the hardest part.

**The big unknown:** how well speech-to-text handles Albanian from a noisy site, in dialect.
Several services support Albanian; before designing anything, test them on real recordings from
the site (Moisi could collect a few).
